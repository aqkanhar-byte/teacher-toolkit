📁 Bulk Upload — many books at once (AI detects Class/Subject for each)

Click the box below, navigate into your books folder, then press Ctrl+A (Windows) or Cmd+A (Mac) to select every file in it, then Open. For each one, the AI reads the first page to detect Class + Subject, and the title comes from the filename — then it's compressed/split/uploaded automatically, one after another, no clicking per book. Anything the AI can't confidently match to your Class/Subject lists is skipped and listed at the end for manual upload.
